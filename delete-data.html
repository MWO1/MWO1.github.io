Delete your data

Hushwater: Merge & Mystery · by Maximilian Olsen · Last updated: 29 September 2026

Hushwater has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is how to remove the data the game keeps.

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Email contact@hushwatergame.com with the subject "Delete my Hushwater data".
  2. If you can, add the date you installed the game and your phone model. This helps us find anonymous play data that belongs to your device.
  3. We reply within 30 days to confirm what was deleted.

Things you can delete yourself right away

  • Game progress on your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
  • Cloud save and leaderboard scores (Android): open your Google Play Games settings, go to Delete Play Games account & data → Delete individual game data and choose Hushwater. This removes the copy of your progress and your scores from your Google account.
  • Anonymous play data and crash reports: turn off Share anonymous play data in the game's Settings. Nothing more is collected after that.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings.
