Privacy Policy

Hushwater: Merge & Mystery · Last updated: 29 September 2026

Who we are: Hushwater is made by Maximilian Olsen ("we", "us"). Contact: contact@hushwatergame.com.

The short version

  • You can play Hushwater without an account.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • If you say yes when asked, we collect anonymous play data and crash reports to improve the game, and our ad partner may show ads based on your interests. You can change both answers any time in Settings.
  • Ads are always optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it for a reward.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Reminders such as "your energy is full" are scheduled on your device. They don't send any data to us, and you can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Your choices

  • Settings → Share anonymous play data and Personalized ads turn these on or off at any time. Turning off play data stops Analytics and crash reports immediately.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form asks for your choices about ads, and you can change them later.
  • On iPhone, the system "Allow tracking?" prompt also controls interest-based ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device. To delete other data, see Delete your data.

Children

Hushwater isn't directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Contact contact@hushwatergame.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Our partners' policies

Google Firebase and AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · GitHub: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data transfers and retention

Our service providers may process data outside your country under standard contractual safeguards. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, crash reports for up to 90 days, and support emails for up to 2 years.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
